# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Artists and designers using a desktop creative tool to explore, combine, and export
dithered image treatments. They work iteratively: adjust a Look, compare changes,
sequence transitions, inspect masking, and export without leaving the visual flow.

## Product Purpose

ditherzam turns still images into expressive dither artwork through a fast,
offline-first workflow. Success means users can understand the current render state,
construct a short Look composition, preview it immediately, and export predictable
results without needing code or a professional video editor.

## Brand Personality

Technical, expressive, immediate.

## Anti-references

- Photoshop-scale complexity that obscures a compact, purpose-built spatial layer
  workflow.
- Generic SaaS dashboards, decorative cards, and ornamental status metrics.
- Oversized controls that reduce usable canvas or timeline space.
- Decorative motion, novelty controls, and interactions that obscure standard
  desktop affordances.
- Interfaces that imply unsupported video masking or hidden cloud processing.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the artwork primary; controls support the canvas rather than competing with it.
2. Make state and scope explicit: users should always know which Look, frame, mask,
   and transition they are editing.
3. Prefer direct manipulation and familiar desktop controls over modal workflows.
4. Preserve responsiveness through asynchronous, latest-wins previews and visible
   progress/error states.
5. Be honest about capability boundaries, especially still-only masking and local
   model availability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators,
color-independent selection/status cues, descriptive control labels, and reduced
motion. Never rely on animation or color alone to communicate timeline state.
